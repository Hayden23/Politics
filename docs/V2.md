# v2: what changed

## Left vs right
- **How each side sees it** (`/stories/:slug/perspectives`, after rating): an editor-approved left and right perspective per story — summary in that side's own terms, strongest arguments (each linked to cited coverage), framing and values emphasized. Stored on the story and versioned with it (`stories.perspectives`, `story_versions.perspectives`; published versions immutable). Perspectives are optional, but **never one-sided**: if either side exists, both must, each with a summary and an argument (blocks review/publish).
- **Outlet lean** (`publishers` table): editors rate outlets left / lean left / center / lean right / right / unrated, and a rating **must state its basis** (e.g. a licensed media-bias rating or documented editorial assessment). Sources are matched to outlets by domain automatically. Readers see lean badges and a **coverage balance** bar on the sources page — after rating. Bulk import: `npm run publishers:import -- file.csv`. No ratings ship with the app.
- **Results by self-identified ideology** (`/stories/:slug/breakdowns`): liberals / moderates / conservatives, from the optional political self-identification, counted **only** for people who consented to demographic processing. Groups under `IDEOLOGY_RESULTS_THRESHOLD` (default 100) are hidden entirely, counts included. A support-gap sentence appears only when both left and right groups qualify.
- AI drafts propose both perspectives; the AI/heuristic **balance check** flags loaded wording, unattributed claims, and left/right asymmetry (length, argument count, uncited arguments).

## P1 analytics
State results (tile-grid map, `STATE_RESULTS_THRESHOLD`), age and area-type breakdowns (`DEMOGRAPHIC_RESULTS_THRESHOLD`), and aggregate opinion-change tracking (changed, toward support/oppose, into/out of uncertainty).

## Engagement
Follow topics (Discover + Profile) → in-app notifications for new briefings; notifications for replies, moderation decisions and appeal outcomes; daily/weekly email digests through `EMAIL_PROVIDER=resend` (otherwise logged and marked skipped); private weekly summary (`/me/summary`).

## Editorial tools
Candidate **clusters** across outlets with lean mix and one-click "promote cluster" (all outlets attached as sources); **scheduled publishing** for approved stories (worker publishes as the scheduling editor, cancelled if they lose editor rights; any edit clears the schedule); readiness **warnings** (one-sided sourcing, unrated outlets, length); publisher registry.

## Design
Desktop two-column story layout with sticky rating, onboarding walkthrough, notification bell, dark-mode contrast pass. Left and right are distinguished by label and position only — never by red/blue or party colors.

## Migration
`npm run db:migrate` applies `0002`/`0003` (additive). Run the worker to get scheduled publishing, digests and email delivery.
